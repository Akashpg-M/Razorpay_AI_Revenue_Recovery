# Product

Revenue leakage is not one retry problem. Customers, payment failures, contact costs, fatigue, natural recovery, and merchant constraints differ. RecoverOS selects the highest expected net recovery value action, including waiting, while preserving explainability and human control.

The product surfaces business impact, full case replay, an escalation queue, resilience experiments, and operational health. Synthetic evaluation supports development comparisons; it is deliberately not described as production causal uplift.
